How to play

A quick reference for gameplay, scoring, and our new question variety system.

Start a game

  1. Choose a duration from 30 seconds to 20 minutes.
  2. Use Settings to choose operations and operand ranges.
  3. Tap Start and type the answer. Correct answers submit automatically.
  4. Keep solving until the timer expires. Your results appear at the end.

Points for each correct answer

Under 3 seconds8 points
3 to under 5 seconds4 points
5 to 7 seconds2 points
Over 7 seconds1 point

No penalty for incorrect or incomplete input. Your clock keeps running until you type the correct answer.

More varied questions in v12.2

The app remembers your last 600 question identities on this browser, even between games and page reloads. It prefers new problems and treats reversed multiplication/addition as repeats (for example, 7 × 8 and 8 × 7).

It still balances your selected operations and uses easy, medium, hard, and very-hard questions in a shuffled 15% / 25% / 30% / 30% distribution. If you select a very narrow number range, repetition can be unavoidable.

History and records

Open History to see your 10 most recent games and the highest score for each duration. Personal bests remain saved when old games disappear from the recent list.

Scores depend on your chosen ranges and operations, so scores from different settings are not always directly comparable.

Offline readiness indicator

The small badge below the app title checks that all required game, icon, and guide files are cached. Ready offline ✓ means the active service worker has verified each file. Tap the badge to recheck or retry downloading missing files while connected to the internet.

The badge does not measure your connection speed or guarantee that iOS will never clear browser storage. Open the app online once after updating to cache the latest version.

Offline and privacy

The game works offline after it is cached on a supported browser. Your settings, records, and recent-question memory stay in local browser storage. Clearing website data also clears those records.

To install the offline app, visit it over HTTPS and use your browser’s Add to Home Screen option where supported.
